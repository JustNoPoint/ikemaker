IKEMAKER 0.79.500 — TESTER BETA NOTES

- Screenpack, select screen and storyboard DEF examples are available in IKEMEN: State Controllers. Use an example on a blank line in its indicated section. Existing scalar keys offer navigation or value replacement.

- Quick sprite intake, Move Lab, Throw Maker, screenpack and storyboard authoring remain available from their existing workspaces.
