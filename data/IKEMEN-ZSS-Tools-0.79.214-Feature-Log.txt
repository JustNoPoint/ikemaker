IKEMEN ZSS Tools 0.79.214

Narrow companion-browser opening policy

- Floating AIR and SFF browsers now open toward the shared 420-pixel minimum width instead of inheriting an oversized auxiliary window.
- The opening adjustment works in both directions, shrinking an overly wide window and enlarging an unusably narrow one.
- After opening settles, a deliberate user resize becomes that browser type's remembered width for later openings.
- AIR and SFF remember their widths independently.
- Embedded and non-floating panels are not resized by the floating-window policy.
- Restored floating AIR browsers reapply the same remembered-width policy.
