IKEMaker 0.74.1

Palette sample drag-and-drop

- Adds a visible drop target for one or more PNG sprite samples.
- Automatically inventories dropped artwork and makes it available to Simple Palette Construction.
- Supports the same automatic palette selection, conversion draft, review, regenerate, revert, and export flow as browsed PNG files.
- Validates PNG contents and enforces a 64 MB webview-drop safety limit; larger sets remain available through Open PNG artwork.
- Keeps all dropped source data read-only and never overwrites the artist's files.
