IKEMaker 0.79.429

Palette Workshop SFF intake and media usability closeout

- Palette Template intake can use the current SFF or a separate SFF without replacing the open character session.
- Indexed, truecolor-only, and mixed SFF paths now lead through explicit palette-family, group, and sprite review.
- Intake retains source identity, axes, authored palette ownership, index-plane evidence, and reversible CS source mapping.
- A live 255-slot budget includes requested separator slots and blocks overflow before extraction.
- Ambiguous palette identities fail closed; identical RGBA indexes are never advertised as preserved when the downstream organizer would merge them.
- Exact indexed CS duplicate removal is palette-family aware and retains the original-to-canonical replacement map.
- AIR and SFF now share pointer-anchored wheel/pinch zoom, canvas-focused plus/minus zoom, and focused Shift+wheel numeric stepping.
- Palette sprite right-click or Shift+F10 provides non-painting color selection and guarded access to the sprite's exact authored palette.
- SND adds stable exact-metadata sorting for duration, rate, channels, stored size, format, and bit depth.

Known bounded capabilities

- Full duplicate-RGBA semantic preservation remains blocked until the palette organizer/export path becomes semantic-index aware; explicit combined-color intake remains available.
- Animated WebP export is not bundled. AIR PNG sequence export and its timing/identity manifest remain the deterministic export path.
