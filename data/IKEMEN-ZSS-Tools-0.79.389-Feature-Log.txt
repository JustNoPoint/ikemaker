IKEMaker 0.79.389

Palette Workshop animation parity

- Added a complete AIR-action chooser to the SFF-backed Palette Workshop.
- Any embedded palette can now be previewed across any neighboring AIR action without first opening SFF Browser.
- Added Replay and Open in AIR Viewer controls beside the animation preview.
- Added a direct PalFX Editor route in the same palette workflow.
- Clarified that animation palette application is a non-destructive preview; AIR and SFF files remain unchanged.
- Prevented stale animation frames from a previously selected palette or action from replacing the current preview.
- Ambiguous duplicate group,index sprite references are reported and skipped rather than resolved arbitrarily.
- Zero, negative, loop, or infinite frame times cannot stall the preview animation.

Astra medium reviewed the Palette Workshop and SFF Browser palette surfaces for parity, accessibility, and ownership. Palette Workshop remains the authoritative palette workspace; other viewers may provide shortcuts but should not own unique palette features.
