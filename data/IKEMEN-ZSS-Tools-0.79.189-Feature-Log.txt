IKEMaker 0.79.189

Character Workbench shared-viewer close repair

- SFF viewers opened from a Character Workbench now persist the exact owning character DEF.
- Shared-template SFF viewers close with their sole owning character even when the archive is not directly assigned in that character DEF.
- A viewer shared by multiple character sessions remains open until its final owner closes.
- SFF viewers intentionally opened standalone remain independent and are not swept into character closure merely because the character references the same file.
- Viewers restored from pre-0.79.189 state receive a one-time explicit adoption choice instead of being guessed or left permanently stuck.
- Session ownership survives VS Code window restoration through versioned webview state.
- Regression coverage includes sole-owner closure, multiple owners, standalone retention, restored-session migration, cancellation, delayed editor settlement, and visual-workspace reporting.
