IKEMaker 0.73.1

Palette Index Organizer opacity diagnostics

- Separately counts exact RGBA entries and underlying base RGB colors.
- Reports how many palette entries exist only because a base color appears at multiple opacity levels.
- Reports the number of base colors with varied alpha and the largest alpha-variant family.
- Explains whether an over-256 source is primarily color-heavy or opacity-heavy.
- Retains the 0.73.0 rule: no silent quantization, alpha flattening, or source overwrite.

The additional diagnostics were prompted by an HD-art palette example where many visually related shades occupied separate slots because of opacity variation.
