IKEMaker 0.64.13
=================

Existing-layout recovery
------------------------
- Character opening reuses an already-empty editor group as the visual workspace instead of creating a third column.
- AIR automatic preview receives the AIR text editor's source column and refuses to use it as the visual destination.
- An AIR viewer inherited from an older layout is moved to the recovered visual group when its AIR text is opened.
- SFF and SND then follow AIR into that same visual group.

The 0.64.12 source/code separation, complete Essentials code set, stale-column reset, and duplicate-archive protection remain in effect.
