IKEMaker 0.77.1 — Retained and Custom Exact Engine Builds

- Adds an explicit Install Retained or Custom Exact Build command.
- The user chooses both a local manifest and its sibling ZIP; browsing and
  preview do not change the project or game.
- Full commit, platform, repository, file size, and SHA-256 are validated. The
  ZIP is rehashed before and after copying to notified temporary staging.
- Bundled exact commit+artifact bindings identify previously reviewed builds;
  a manifest cannot certify itself.
- Any exact custom fork can proceed through a separate unsupported-build
  override and is recorded as custom/user-asserted with runtime evidence unknown.
- Retained catalog IDs are derived from repository, full commit, platform, and
  full artifact digest; imported IDs are display claims only. Custom override
  variants additionally bind the preserved files' content hashes.
- Compatibility/review gates may be overridden. Archive path/link protection,
  protected content, backup, journaling, rollback, and live-change checks cannot.
- Retained builds use the same migration pipeline as online Nightlies, remain
  pinned, and never authorize automatic future upgrades.
