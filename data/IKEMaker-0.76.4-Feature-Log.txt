IKEMaker 0.76.4 — Viewer/text placement

- A visual workspace first opens in the shared viewer group beside text code.
- Move Lab now follows the same placement contract as AIR, SFF, SND, HitDef, and the other visual workspaces.
- Once a viewer is open, other viewers reuse that viewer group.
- A user can still drag viewer tabs elsewhere; IKEMaker respects the most recently activated viewer location.
