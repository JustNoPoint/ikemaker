IKEMaker 0.79.405

Layered palette-template PSD stack correction

- Places the opaque transparent-color/canvas preview at the bottom of the PSD
  layer stack instead of appending it above the artwork.
- Keeps color/index 0 blank (alpha zero) inside every sprite layer.
- Places imported sprites and named swatch layers above the background.
- Keeps manual separator lines above the artwork.
- Names the base layer "Canvas background (bottom layer)" so its purpose and
  intended position are unambiguous when the PSD opens in an external editor.
