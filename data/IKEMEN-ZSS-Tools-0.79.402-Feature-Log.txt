IKEMaker 0.79.402 - separator-chain Undo and Redo

The connected image-template separator tool now has its own retained history.
Undo removes an entire completed multi-segment chain in one step; Redo restores
that complete chain. Clearing all separator lines is also one undoable action.

Local Undo and Redo buttons appear beside the separator controls. Ctrl+Z
performs Undo. Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z both perform Redo. Native editing
shortcuts inside text, number, and selection fields remain untouched.

History is bounded to 30 exact before/after checkpoints, rejects empty Undo or
Redo operations, and revalidates the current template canvas before applying a
checkpoint. Regression coverage verifies atomic chain Undo/Redo, both common
Redo shortcuts, empty-history safety, and the existing connected-line flow.
