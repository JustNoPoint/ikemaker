IKEMaker 0.79.312

Three-choice warning for new colors in retained cleanup regions

- Cleanup-region coordinates remain active as more template source images are
  added instead of being lost when the collective inventory changes.
- If a newly added image introduces new exact colors inside a retained cleanup
  region, Template Creator stops before removing any of those new pixels.
- The blocking warning explains that a sprite may overlap the retained region
  and offers exactly three choices:
  - Ignore the new colors: leave the new image pixels and palette colors intact.
  - Remove the new colors going forward: globally exclude every exact match of
    the newly introduced colors.
  - Continue deleting the selected area: spatially clear the retained region
    in affected new images while preserving matching colors outside it.
- The warning is tied to the exact collective inventory and cannot be applied
  after its sources change.
- The selected decision is retained in continuation history, and unresolved
  warnings block contact approval and organization.
- Original PNG files remain unchanged for all three choices.

Verification

- Added controller coverage for warning detection and all three resolutions.
- Verified ignore preserves new colors, global removal marks them ignored, and
  spatial continuation adds masks while retaining matching outside pixels.
