IKEMaker 0.71.3 — GIF timing to IKEMEN tick conversion

- GIF timings are interpreted as elapsed time, not as a one-frame/one-tick format.
- Normal GIF timing uses cumulative 60 Hz quantization to prevent independent
  per-frame rounding from accumulating unnecessary drift.
- Frames shorter than one 60 Hz game tick are detected before AIR generation.
- The user must choose between:
  1. Resample to 60 Hz, preserving approximate total duration while allowing
     unsampled source frames to be omitted.
  2. Preserve every source frame at a minimum of one tick, accepting that the
     resulting IKEMEN animation must be longer than the GIF.
- Generated AIR comments record which timing policy was used.
- The completion notice reports the resulting AIR element or tick count.
- Unreliable or missing GIF delays cannot be treated as timing; uniform timing
  must be selected instead.
- Artist Intake and Two-SFF Assembly remain SFF-only and never invoke this logic.

Verification: all 142 automated test files pass.
