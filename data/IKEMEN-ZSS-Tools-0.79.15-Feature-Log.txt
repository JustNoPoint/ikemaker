IKEMaker 0.79.15 — AIR frame-edit navigation recovery

- Accepted frame-field edits release the guarded AIR form before Auto Save begins.
- A delayed or failed disk save can no longer permanently block frame or animation selection.
- Successful frame edits explicitly clear the pending timer and touched-field state.
- Failed frame edits remain visible and recoverable instead of silently discarding the typed value.
- Auto Save still writes the exact open AIR document and reports confirmed disk persistence separately.
