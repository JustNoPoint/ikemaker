IKEMaker 0.77.2
================

Visual coordinate editing
-------------------------
- JNP Move Constants: drag the yellow hit-spark marker to draft the active
  window's sparkX/sparkY pair. Multi-hit windows retain their own spark2,
  spark3, and later pairs. Source code changes only through the existing
  explicit Apply buttons.
- Universal HitDef Editor: drag the yellow spark marker to draft sparkxy,
  then use Apply to Code.
- Helper Lab: drag the amber spawn marker or projectile sprite to draft Spawn
  X/Y. Hold Shift while dragging to pan.
- Explod Composer and Position & Camera were audited and already provide
  direct marker dragging with Shift-drag panning.

Contact-result quick editing
----------------------------
- The JNP contact-result card now edits move-local damage, ground hitstun,
  ground slide time, guard stun, and the applicable Counter/Punish values.
- The card shows inherited chip damage, pause times, air hit time, ground,
  guard and air velocities, and Y acceleration when the assigned game profile
  exposes them.
- Inherited profile values are labeled shared and open their exact source.
  This prevents a game-wide light/medium/heavy profile edit from appearing to
  be a one-move change.
- The UI explains that HitDef has no independent guard.slidetime: guard motion
  is governed by guard.hittime and guard.velocity.

Safety
------
- Dragging updates retained drafts; it does not silently rewrite gameplay
  source.
- Existing panning, opponent positioning, zoom, Undo, source-conflict checks,
  and explicit apply workflows remain intact.
