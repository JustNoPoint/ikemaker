IKEMaker 0.79.277

AIR animated thumbnails

- Added an optional Animate thumbnails control to the AIR Actions Browser.
- Animation is off by default and the choice is remembered for the current AIR browser.
- Only thumbnail cards entering the visible browser area request their frame images.
- Playback follows positive AIR frame times while giving zero, negative, and infinite-time frames a short visible preview duration.
- Thumbnail previews always cycle through the complete Action; AIR loop points and infinite holds cannot trap the preview.
- List view remains text-only and does not start thumbnail playback.
