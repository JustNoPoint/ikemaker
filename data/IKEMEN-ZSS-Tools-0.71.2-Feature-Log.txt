IKEMaker 0.71.2 — SFF/AIR authority correction

SFF-ONLY INTAKE AND ASSEMBLY
- Artist Intake creates no AIR draft and reads no GIF timing.
- Two-SFF Assembly changes no AIR file.
- Package metadata records scope SFF_ONLY and the policy to preserve the existing
  destination/template AIR unchanged.
- Sprite identity, grouping, naming metadata, axes, palettes, provenance, and the
  reviewed SprMaker2 build remain the only responsibilities of these workflows.

DELIBERATE GIF TO AIR
- The AIR viewer now contains a separate GIF → AIR action.
- The user chooses the destination action, SFF group, first sprite index, and timing.
- Compilation GIFs default to uniform one-tick elements for later AIR authoring.
- Uniform custom timing is available.
- GIF-delay timing is available only through an explicit choice and only when every
  frame exposes a reliable delay.
- Existing AIR actions require explicit replacement confirmation.
- Missing mapped SFF identities are disclosed before the AIR mapping is created.
- The AIR editor changes without automatic saving; SFF data is never changed.

VERIFICATION
- GIF/AIR generation, appending, replacement, and timing-choice behavior have pure
  model coverage.
- The complete automated suite passes: 142 test files.
