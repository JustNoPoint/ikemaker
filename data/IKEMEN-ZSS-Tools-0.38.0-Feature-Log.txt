IKEMEN ZSS Tools 0.38.0
=======================

Advanced shortcuts and bulk access
----------------------------------
- Added compact Advanced-only shortcut bars to SFF, AIR, SND, stage,
  screenpack/fight UI, and command/movelist workspaces.
- SFF exposes next-unresolved review, batch axis offset, palettes, and save.
- AIR exposes batch Clsn2, collision, runtime geometry, and source inspection.
- SND exposes marked export, split-contract audit, build manifest, and rebuild.
- Stage and screenpack expose their common navigation, diagnostics, preview,
  mirroring, and reviewed position operations.
- Commands expose add, search/filter, reviewed apply, and movelist access.
- Every shortcut activates an existing control; no alternate mutation or save
  path was introduced.
- Learning mode omits the compact bar and retains the guided checklist order.
