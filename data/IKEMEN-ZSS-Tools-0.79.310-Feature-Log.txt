IKEMaker 0.79.310

Template Creator shared-color removal choice

- Every color that also occurs outside a selected cleanup region now presents
  two explicit choices:
  - Remove only inside this selection.
  - Remove all instances of this color.
- The selection-only choice is the default and preserves matching sprite pixels
  everywhere outside the selected rectangle.
- Global removal is opt-in per exact shared RGBA color and clearly states that
  it affects every imported image.
- Colors exclusive to the selected region remain automatic because clearing the
  region removes their final working-image occurrence.
- Original PNG files remain unchanged under either choice.
