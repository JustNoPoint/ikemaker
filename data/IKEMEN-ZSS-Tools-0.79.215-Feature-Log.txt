IKEMEN ZSS Tools 0.79.215

Training launch crash fix

- Training launches no longer combine `-loadmotif` with `-training`, which crashed the current IKEMEN nightly in `menu.lua`.
- Current-character Training, configured Training, saved Training presets, and IKEMaker test/diagnostic sessions now use the safe Training-only launch path.
- Quick VS keeps its existing game-mode `-loadmotif` launch behavior.
- Match Setup now previews the same safe arguments that will actually be launched.
- A shared launch guard and regression coverage prevent later Training entry points from restoring the crashing combination.
