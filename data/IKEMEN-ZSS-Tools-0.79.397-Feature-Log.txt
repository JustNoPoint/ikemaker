IKEMaker 0.79.397 — Deep palette-bank viewport ownership

Use stable bank/card/editor identities for deep Palette Workshop anchors,
including data-bank-card, data-bank-grid, body/section identities and stable IDs.
Do not anchor to the sticky preview or any of its descendants: their viewport
position cannot measure movement of the document underneath them.

Retain the original semantic anchor across rapid passive updates while the
asynchronous bank editor is temporarily absent. Do not recapture a different
heading or loading placeholder midway through rebuilding the same editor.

Chromium CSP regression positions Bank A at y=120, replaces the real bank editor
through enhanceRefresh(), inserts late header content, and waits 1500ms. The
packaged 0.79.396 runtime fails with Bank A moving to y=317. This version retains
y=120, including after five rapid session rebuilds and deferred enhancements.
