IKEMaker 0.79.94

- Packages the shared dropdown menu bar that was completed after the earlier
  0.79.93 package was built.
- Adds contextual Project, Edit, View, Palettes, Sprites, Animations, Sounds,
  Review, Build, Tools, and Help menus to SFF, AIR, and SND.
- Keeps the established viewer buttons available during hands-on testing.
- Uses an explicit host-side command allowlist and existing viewer controls;
  unavailable actions are disabled rather than silently redirected.
- Adds keyboard navigation, action search, favorites, recent actions, safe
  repeat, and clear descriptions of what supported actions change.
- Opening, searching, or navigating menus does not modify project files.
- The complete 405-file automated source suite passes. Installed visual and
  interaction testing remains required.
