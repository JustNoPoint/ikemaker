IKEMaker 0.79.419

Palette Workshop template colors

- Added a visible Deselect all colors button beside the selected-color viewing controls.
- The button reports the current selection count and disables itself when nothing is selected.
- Renamed Clear focus to Stop darken/lighten so stopping the visual effect is distinct from clearing the selected colors.
- Deselect all colors uses the existing authoritative clear-selection action and also removes the active darken/lighten preview.
- Individual selected colors can still be deselected by clicking them again.
