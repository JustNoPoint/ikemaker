IKEMaker 0.73.0

PALETTE INDEX ORGANIZER

- Creates a reusable semantic color-separation/index contract from one or more
  PNG sources or an existing SFF palette.
- Counts exact RGBA colors, alpha levels, fully transparent colors, and
  semi-transparent colors. Different opacity values are correctly treated as
  separate palette entries.
- Explains when HD/true-color art cannot fit safely into 256 indexed colors.
  IKEMaker does not silently quantize, flatten alpha, or pretend the conversion
  is lossless.
- Clicking source artwork selects the exact underlying RGBA color.
- Colors can be classified as transparency, outline, skin, hair, eyes,
  clothing, equipment, shared FX, semi-transparent FX, or a custom semantic
  region, with an optional shade/usage label.
- Exact conversions reserve transparency at index 0 and keep duplicate-looking
  colors separate unless the source is deliberately changed by a person.
- Exports JSON and CSV index contracts, indexed swatch PNG, ACT, Photoshop ACO,
  Aseprite GPL, and exact indexed copies of every source PNG.
- Original artwork and SFF archives are never overwritten.
- Available from Create New, SFF and Sprites, and directly from the SFF palette
  workspace and Artist Intake bridges.
