IKEMaker 0.79.9 — Critical AIR viewer initialization hotfix

- Corrects the 0.79.8 AIR page-assembly order that could omit required AIR-only
  controls after destination-color attributes were applied.
- AIR viewer startup now completes and applies its animation model instead of
  stopping on a missing-control binding.
- Open SFF retains its AIR viewer click binding and the approved green SFF
  destination identity.
- Generated-page regression coverage now requires the shared viewer toolbar,
  AIR connected-viewer route, GIF-to-AIR and AIR Log controls, and Open SFF
  binding to exist together.
- No AIR, SFF, character, stage, or game content migration is required.
- The complete 325-file source test suite passes.
- Resizable viewer height and universal whole-editor vertical scrolling are
  recorded future requirements and are not claimed by this hotfix.
