Image template intake and authority

- Separates four clear starting paths: edit the current primary template, import a recolored copy, review an expanded copy, or review a completely new template candidate.
- Keeps the primary indexed project template authoritative and unchanged during every external-image intake.
- Uses reviewed swatch-zone metadata as an overlay for same-canvas recolored submissions, including true-color copies, and saves only a separate recovered indexed copy.
- Routes changed-canvas recolors to the explicit expanded-template path instead of silently treating them as ordinary palette submissions.
- Keeps expanded and replacement candidates isolated from the current template artwork; the two images are never merged onto one construction canvas.
- Indexed replacement candidates enter review. True-color replacement or expansion candidates expose only Manual Repair or Reject.
- Requires reviewed color coverage, swatch positions, row names, index order, a canonical indexed candidate, and explicit final approval before a candidate can become primary.
- Promotion policy archives the prior primary and records either a reviewed primary expansion update or a reviewed complete replacement.
- Adds stale-review hashes and exact source evidence to prevent an intake or promotion decision from being replayed after its files or authority change.
