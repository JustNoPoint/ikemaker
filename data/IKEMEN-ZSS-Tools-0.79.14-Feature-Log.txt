IKEMaker 0.79.14 — AIR frame selection and disk persistence

- Selecting an AIR timeline frame now pauses playback and retains the chosen frame.
- Previous Frame and Next Frame use the same guarded selection path.
- Genuine partial frame edits and pending writes remain protected.
- With IKEMaker Auto Save enabled, accepted visual AIR edits save the exact AIR document to disk once.
- The viewer reports confirmed disk persistence or an actionable save failure.
- With IKEMaker Auto Save disabled, Save AIR remains the explicit disk-write control.
- Saving AIR does not claim that a running IKEMEN match has reloaded the character.
