IKEMaker 0.79.423

Layered template PSD updates

- A layered template PSD may now be saved over its existing filename after explicit confirmation.
- The sibling IKEMaker setup file is updated together with the PSD as one paired operation.
- Both existing destinations are hash-checked after confirmation; an external change cancels the replacement.
- The paired write is transactional, so failure restores the prior pair instead of leaving a mismatched PSD and setup file.
- Choosing a different filename still creates a new PSD and setup pair normally.

Also includes the 0.79.422 unified color drag-and-drop repair.
