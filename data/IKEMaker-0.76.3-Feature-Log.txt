IKEMaker 0.76.3 — Universal attack discovery

- Move Lab now discovers attacks from the character's authored CNS/ZSS HitDef controllers.
- Every detected attack is listed even when the character has only one.
- Selecting an entry opens that exact HitDef in the visual HitDef editor.
- JNP Move Constants are presented separately as optional project-specific modular profiles.
- Direct-HitDef creators are not required or encouraged to adopt Move Constants.
