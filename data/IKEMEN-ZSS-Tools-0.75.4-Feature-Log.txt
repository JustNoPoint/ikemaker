IKEMaker 0.75.4

- SFF and SND custom editors now explicitly activate when Windows or VS Code
  opens those file types directly.
- A startup fallback detects SFF/SND files received before extension activation
  and reopens them in the proper visual workspace.
- AIR retains its intended editable-text plus synchronized-viewer behavior.
- DEF, ZSS, CNS, INP/CMD, and Lua remain text-first by design.
