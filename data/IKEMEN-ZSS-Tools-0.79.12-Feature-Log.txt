IKEMaker 0.79.12 — AIR animation navigation recovery

- AIR actions and frames can be selected while recoverable Runtime-frame drafts exist.
- Those drafts remain stored against their original AIR action and frame; navigation does not discard or move them.
- An actively saving Runtime plan still temporarily blocks navigation to prevent overlapping changes.
- Genuine unfinished frame-field and collision-box edits retain their existing protections.
- Regression coverage verifies that saved Runtime drafts cannot trap the viewer on one animation.
