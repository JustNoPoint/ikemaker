IKEMaker 0.79.408

Fast image-session restoration

- Removes the full cached-pixel rescan that previously rebuilt cleanup-adjusted color counts after loading an image session.
- Cleanup masks now subtract only the pixels inside their reviewed rectangles from the already-saved per-image color counts, including correct union handling for overlapping masks.
- An existing template canvas now prepares only the image layers actually retained in its saved layout. Intake checkboxes may still contain every imported image without forcing all of them through canvas preparation.
- Loading an image session from an SFF-owned Palette Workshop no longer constructs and publishes the ordinary folder-intake projection before restoring the saved session.
- The contact page is restored first. Template canvas image preparation is deferred until that canvas is opened.
- Verified against the 1,502-image vsav2000 session: cleanup-adjusted contact reconstruction reduced from over 30 seconds to roughly 2.9 seconds, and its 23-layer canvas reduced from over 30 seconds to roughly 1.7 seconds on the same machine.
- Adds regression coverage for cached count subtraction, overlapping cleanup masks, SFF-owned image-session routing, and saved-canvas layer authority.
