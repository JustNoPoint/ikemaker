IKEMaker 0.75.6

PROTECTED PALETTE BACKGROUND

- JustNoPoint's provisional CS background/index-0 RGB is #00F5BA regardless of
  the color used by incoming artwork.
- Phase 1 exports rewrite the transparent entry RGB to the configured protected
  color while keeping it transparent in the indexed contract.
- Exact and near-matching visible sprite colors receive deterministic, distinct
  provisional RGB replacements. Their original RGBA values and the collision
  decision remain recorded for review and reversal.
- The default similarity guard is RGB distance 24.
- Other users may change the background color and threshold per workspace using
  IKEMaker settings. This does not change JNP project policy.
- Opaque artwork that cannot distinguish flat background pixels from same-color
  visible sprite pixels requires a reviewed mask or transparent source; the
  tool must not erase every occurrence by assumption.
