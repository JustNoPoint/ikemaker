IKEMaker 0.79.421

Template color focus controls

- Darken unselected and Lighten unselected are now always available.
- Either viewing mode can be enabled before selecting any colors; it activates as soon as colors are selected.
- Fixed retained palette drafts keeping a stale focus callback after a protected background refresh.
- The active draft now restores its own Darken/Lighten behavior after canvas or layout updates.
- Status text distinguishes an armed mode awaiting color selection from an active visual effect.
