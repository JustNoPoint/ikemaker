IKEMaker 0.79.412 — Edge-reaching template separators

- The palette-template separator tool now has a visible drawing gutter outside the working canvas.
- A separator chain may begin outside the canvas and finish by double-clicking outside it.
- Outside endpoints do not enlarge the editable or exported canvas.
- When the template is finalized, marked outside endpoints extend to the finished image edge, including the fresh 10-pixel background margin.
- Connected interior turns retain their exact authored positions.
- Added regression coverage for horizontal and diagonal edge intersections, fixed canvas sizing, and the outside-canvas gestures.
