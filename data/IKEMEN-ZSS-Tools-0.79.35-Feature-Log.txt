IKEMaker 0.79.35 — Parallel AIR docking workspace experiment

- Adds a separate Open AIR Docking Workspace (Experimental) route.
- Leaves the original AIR viewer, auto-open, editing, autosave, and mutation paths unchanged.
- Provides read-only Animations, Canvas, Inspector, and Timeline panels that can
  be docked and tabbed internally in either a normal editor tab or floating window.
- Includes Recommended and optional Bottom Tools layouts, Return to Main Window,
  native source reveal, narrow-window stacking, and workspace layout saving.
