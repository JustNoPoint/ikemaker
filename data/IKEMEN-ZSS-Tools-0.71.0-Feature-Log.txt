IKEMaker 0.71.0 — Artist intake and two-SFF assembly

ARTIST INTAKE
- Accepts animation GIFs, PNG sprite sheets, and folders of loose PNG frames.
- Extracts into a temporary working area without changing artist originals.
- Preserves source files, source labels, frame order, and available GIF delays as provenance.
- Ignores GIF/source timing by default; the draft uses neutral one-tick browsing frames
  labeled timing-unassigned. REFERENCE_ONLY timing requires explicit opt-in.
- Detects exact duplicate frames and shows their first matching frame.
- Supports visual multi-frame selection and editable category, name, group/index,
  and axis assignment.
- Suggests broad IKEMaker group families while leaving every assignment editable.
- Writes manifest.csv, artist-intake.json, import-recipe.json, draft.air, working
  PNGs, preserved sources, and a SprMaker2 package for a temporary review SFF.

TWO-SFF ASSEMBLY
- Opens one destination/template SFF and one source SFF in one visual workspace.
- Supports whole-group drag and Ctrl/Shift-selected sprite drag.
- Adds at the next free destination indexes or explicitly replaces matching IDs.
- Can preserve destination axes during replacement.
- Never silently overwrites an identity: unresolved collisions stop the package.
- Extracts a complete destination build, records every operation and source
  identity, preserves the destination palette table, and prepares SprMaker2 files.

SAFETY AND SCOPE
- This is asset intake and assembly, not an image editor.
- Source files are preserved and archive mutation stays on the reviewed manifest,
  transaction, palette, backup, and SprMaker2 authority path.
- Browser hosts expose honest unavailable-command guidance; native extraction and
  archive building remain desktop-only.

VERIFICATION
- New pure-model coverage validates sheet slicing, naming/group suggestions,
  timing authority, selection, append behavior, replacement, axis retention, and
  collision blocking.
- The complete automated suite passes: 141 test files.
