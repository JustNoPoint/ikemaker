AIR SFF Sprite Explorer

- Adds a clear SFF Sprites tab to the AIR Actions Browser.

- Select individual sprites or an entire visible group in exact playback order.
- Create a new AIR animation from the selected sprites with action number, name/comment, and default timing.
- Append the selected sprites to the currently selected AIR Action.
- Shows the exact AIR block or frame lines before the reviewed edit begins.
- Uses exact physical SFF sprite identities while selecting and preserves the selected order.
- Loads sprite thumbnails only for the visible group to keep the narrow browser responsive.
- Keeps all AIR changes on the existing guarded, stale-source-aware, undoable action-edit path.
