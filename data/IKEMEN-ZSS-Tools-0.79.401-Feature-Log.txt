IKEMaker 0.79.401 - connected template separator chains

The image-template separator tool now remains active across turns. The first
click starts a chain, each following single click adds a connected segment,
and double-click finishes and saves the entire chain as one retained edit.

Escape or right-click cancels the unfinished chain. The live preview follows
the pointer, committed draft segments stay visible while drawing, and every
segment retains the selected thickness and darkest named-swatch-group color.

The host validates the exact current canvas, color group, bounds, segment
lengths, thickness and 512-segment template limit before accepting the chain.
Regression coverage checks connected turns, double-click completion, both
cancel gestures, atomic submission, bounded coordinates and zero-length
rejection.
