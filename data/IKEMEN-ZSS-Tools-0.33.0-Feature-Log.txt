IKEMEN ZSS Tools 0.33.0
=======================

SFF mutation authority and safety
---------------------------------
- Classified SFF actions as direct archive edits, SprMaker2 manifest edits,
  rebuild outputs, or non-destructive exports.
- Migrated direct axis edits, batch axis offsets, group-header renaming,
  palette assignments, palette color edits, palette reversal, palette ACT
  replacement, and Save As to transactional binary replacement.
- Direct SFF edits reject stale loaded archives and do not decode or re-encode
  sprite image data.
- Migrated SprMaker2 manifest axis, group, add, remove, duplicate, replacement,
  classification, axis-copy, layer, part, and dismissal edits.
- Manifest saves remember the version that was read and reject later external
  changes instead of silently replacing them.
- Import from another SFF commits extracted PNGs and the destination manifest
  as one aggregate transaction.
- Layer-source backup moves are validated first and reversed when the manifest
  update fails.
- Migrated SFF review metadata, requirement profiles, and alias-registry
  creation to the shared mutation service.
- SFF headers now distinguish direct archive/manifest saving from the separate
  SprMaker2 rebuild authority and show backup/history policy.
- Export-only PNG, aligned image, channel, and ACT outputs remain exports and
  do not claim archive mutation authority.

Verification
------------
- Added a real-file stale-manifest preservation test.
- All 50 extension test files pass.
